Layout Guide

Vault Layout Optimized

Fallout Shelter — Endgame Optimization

Guard Room (Lv1)
1st room after Vault Door. Shallow room (Lv1) = instant positioning. 6 Dwellers with Dragon's Maw / MIRV / Vengeance.
Nuclear Reactor (Max)
2 rooms = power for the entire vault. Workers with S10. Also serve as kill zone.
Nuka-Cola Bottler (Max)
2 rooms = food + water for 200 Dwellers. Workers with E+I max.
Workshops (Max)
Weapon + Outfit. Craft Legendary weapons and outfits.
Overseer's Office (fixed 6 sq)
Required for Quests. Lv3 = 3 simultaneous quests. One per vault. No staff.
Training Rooms
One per SPECIAL stat. E-training ISOLATED (gaps on both sides).
Cap Farm (Lv1)
Medbay/SciLab 1x alternating. 2 high-Luck Dwellers per room. Infinite rush.
Storage + Living (bottom)
No staff. Gaps between rooms = incidents die on their own.
E-Training (Isolated!)
Lv1 Dwellers are fragile. No adjacent rooms = zero incident spread.

Critical Rules

  • Never upgrade Vault Door — only increases incident duration. Cosmetic themes work at Lv1.
  • SPECIAL does not affect vault combat — only Weapon Damage and max HP matter. Outfits are irrelevant for defense.
  • Max HP — every Dweller must be leveled Lv1→50 with Endurance 10 + E7 outfit equipped. This yields 595 HP (maximum possible).
  • Elevators on both edges — blocks Molerat spawns in all production rooms.
  • Deathclaws die on floors 2-3 — 18 armed Dwellers on kill floors eliminate any invader before floor 4.
  • Radio Room off — attracts Raiders/Deathclaws unnecessarily. Babies > wasteland recruits.
  • Mr. Handy — one per production floor (2-3). DO NOT place on floor 1 to prevent him from greeting invaders.
  • Breeding — equip twins pet BEFORE pregnancy, switch to Child-SPECIAL pet before birth. Combined parent SPECIAL 134+ = Legendary baby.
  • Overseer's Office — required for Quests (main source of Legendary loot). Upgrade to Lv3 = 3 simultaneous quests. One per vault.
  • Narrower surface — the top row has only 17 squares (vs 20+ underground). Vault Door (6 sq) + Guard Room 3-wide (9 sq) + Elevator fills the entire width.
