Layout Guide

GOLDEN RULES — follow ALWAYS

  • Vault Door Lv1 — NEVER upgrade. Cosmetic themes work at Lv1
  • Guard Room (Storage) Lv1 — NEVER upgrade. Shallow room = faster combat
  • HP is PERMANENT — calculated on level-up. No way to fix afterwards. E17 (E10 + outfit +7E) from Lv1 to 50 = 595 HP (maximum)
  • E Protocol (from 35dw): every new dweller trains E to 10 + equips +E outfit BEFORE gaining any XP
  • Elevators on edges of all underground floors — blocks Molerat spawns
  • DO NOT build Radio Studio (20dw) — attracts Raiders and Deathclaws unnecessarily
  • SPECIAL does not affect combat in the vault — only weapon damage + dweller HP matter
  • Empty rooms generate incidents — do not build unnecessary rooms
BUILD ORDER — STEP BY STEP
Adjust the Dwellers count at the top of the page to see your current milestone. Completed milestones are dimmed. Click to expand/collapse.
0-5 DW

TUTORIAL + INITIAL SETUP

▼
  • 1 Complete the tutorial normally — it guides you to build the first rooms (PG, Diner, LQ)
  • 2 After the tutorial, build an Elevator on the RIGHT edge of Floor 1 (surface)
  • 3 Extend the elevator down to Floor 2
  • 4 Build a Diner (1 block) to the left of the elevator on Floor 2
  • 5 Build Water Treatment (1 block) to the left of the Diner
  • 6 Build Living Quarters (1 block) — for breeding
  • 7 Breeding: 1 male + 1 female in the LQ. Repeat until 12 dwellers
  • 8 Explore with extra dwellers (caps + weapons + outfits)
  • STAFF S → Power Gen  |  A → Diner  |  P → Water Treatment
  • TIP Save caps for the 12dw milestone (guard room). Rush at <40% fail chance for extra caps
  • TIP DO NOT spend lunchboxes on room upgrades. Save for rare items and dwellers
6-11 DW

BASIC EXPANSION

▼
  • 1 Expand Power Generator to 2 blocks (merge) on Floor 1 or 2
  • 2 Build an elevator on the LEFT edge of Floor 2 (blocks Molerats)
  • 3 Expand Diner and Water as needed (merge to 2 blocks)
  • 4 Continue breeding — goal: reach 12 dwellers fast!
  • 5 Send dwellers with good weapons to explore (caps + items)
  • 6 Rush rooms at <40% fail chance for extra caps
Floor 2 goal: [ELEV_L]→[LQ]→[Water]→[Diner]→[PG]→[ELEV_R]
  • WARNING DO NOT build unnecessary rooms — every empty room generates incidents on its own
12 DW

GUARD ROOM — STORAGE ROOM UNLOCKED

▼
MOST IMPORTANT EARLY GAME MILESTONE! The Storage Room becomes your permanent guard room on Floor 1.
  • 1 Make sure PG + Diner + Water are working on Floor 2 (don't depend on Floor 1)
  • 2 DESTROY the rooms between VD and the Elevator on Floor 1 (tutorial PGs/Diners)
  • 3 Build a Storage Room (1 block, ~150 caps) right after the VD
  • 4 Build +2 Storage Rooms adjacent (merge to 3 blocks = complete guard room, ~300 caps)
  • 5 Place 6 dwellers with BEST WEAPONS in the guard room
Floor 1: [VD]→[Storage 3 blocks Lv1]→[ELEV]
  • LV1 NEVER upgrade the Storage Room — keep Lv1 ALWAYS (shallow room = fast combat)
  • TIP SPECIAL does not affect vault combat — only weapon damage + HP matter
14-16 DW

MEDBAY + SCIENCE LAB

▼
  • 14dw Build Medbay (1 block, ~150 caps) — produces Stimpaks
    Place wherever there's space. Final position: Floor 9 (3-wide). Staff with high I.
  • 16dw Build Science Lab (1 block, ~150 caps) — produces RadAway
    Place wherever there's space. Final position: Floors 10-11 (cap farm). Staff with high I.
  • USE Give Stimpaks + RadAway to explorers — increases Wasteland time
18 DW

OVERSEER'S OFFICE — QUESTS

▼
  • 1 Build Overseer's Office (2 fixed blocks, ~150 caps)
    Final position: Floor 5 (next to Weight Room which unlocks at 24dw)
  • 2 Upgrade to Lv3 (allows sending 3 simultaneous quests)
  • 3 Start doing quests IMMEDIATELY — main source of Legendary weapons and outfits
  • TIP No staff needed to function. Prioritize quests that give Legendary weapons
  • UNIQUE You can only have 1 OO per vault
20 DWELLERS — DO NOT BUILD RADIO STUDIO! Attracts Raiders and Deathclaws unnecessarily. Breeding is better for recruiting (babies inherit parent stats). If you built it by accident, destroy it.
22-34 DW

WORKSHOPS + TRAINING I

▼
  • 22dw Weapon Workshop (3 fixed blocks) — Floor 4, left side. Weapon crafting
  • 24dw Weight Room (1-3 blocks, trains S) — Floor 5, next to OO. Merge to 3 blocks
  • 26dw Athletics Room (1-3 blocks, trains A) — Floor 8
  • 28dw Armory (1-3 blocks, trains P) — Floor 6
  • 30dw Classroom (1-3 blocks, trains I) — Floor 8, next to Athletics. Merge to 3 blocks
  • 32dw Outfit Workshop (3 fixed blocks) — Floor 4, next to Weapon WS. Outfit crafting
  • PRIOR Training priority: S (power) and I (Stimpaks/RadAway) first
  • CRAFT Prioritize crafting S-tier weapons (Dragon's Maw, MIRV, Vengeance) and +7E outfits
35 DW

FITNESS ROOM — E PROTOCOL (MOST IMPORTANT IN THE GAME)

▼
THIS IS THE MOST IMPORTANT MILESTONE IN THE ENTIRE GAME. From now on, all new dwellers must follow the E Protocol before doing ANYTHING else.
  • 1 Build Fitness Room (1-3 blocks, trains E) on Floor 7
    Layout: ELEV → [gap] → Fitness 3 blocks → [gap] → ELEV. ISOLATED!
  • 2 GAPS on both sides — Lv1 dwellers training E are FRAGILE. Isolation prevents incident spread
E PROTOCOL — for ALL new dwellers:
  • 1 Born or arrives → goes STRAIGHT to Fitness Room
  • 2 Train E to 10
  • 3 Equip outfit with +E (ideally +7E for E17 total)
  • 4 ONLY THEN gain XP (work, explore, train other stats)
  • HP HP = 10 + (E + outfitE - 1) × 0.5 per level up. E17 from Lv1→50 = 595 HP (game maximum)
  • WARNING Dwellers that already leveled up WITHOUT high E have permanently low HP. No way to fix
  • TIP Pre-35 dwellers (before Fitness Room) will be your initial workforce. They'll eventually be replaced
40-50 DW

TRAINING II + CAP FARM SETUP

▼
  • 40dw Lounge (trains C) — Floor 6, next to Armory
  • 42dw Theme Workshop (3 fixed blocks) — optional, cosmetics
  • 45dw Game Room (trains L) — Floor 9, next to the 3-wide production Medbay
  • 50dw Barbershop (2 fixed blocks) — optional, cosmetics
CAP FARM SETUP (Floors 10-11):
  • 1 Build Medbay single (1 block) Lv1 alternating with Science Lab
  • 2 Pattern: Med-Sci-Med-Sci-Med-Sci (6 rooms per floor)
  • 3 2 dwellers per room (high Luck + high Level)
  • 4 Infinite rush — high Luck = high success rate = fast caps
  • LV1 DO NOT upgrade cap farm rooms — Lv1 for fast cycle
  • MERGE DO NOT merge — keep as singles. Med-Sci alternation prevents auto-merge
60-100 DW

ENDGAME — PRODUCTION UPGRADES

▼
  • 60dw Nuclear Reactor replaces Power Generator on Floor 2
    Destroy PGs → build 2× NR 3 blocks. Staff with S10. 2 maxed = power for 200dw
  • 70dw Garden replaces Diner on Floor 3
    Temporary — will be replaced by Nuka-Cola Bottler at 100dw
  • 80dw Water Purification replaces Water Treatment on Floor 3
    Temporary — will be replaced by Nuka-Cola Bottler at 100dw
  • 100dw Nuka-Cola Bottler replaces EVERYTHING on Floor 3
    2× NCB 3 blocks maxed = food AND water for 200 dwellers. FINAL upgrade
Floor 2 final: [ELEV]→[Nuclear Reactor 3b]→[Nuclear Reactor 3b]→[ELEV]
Floor 3 final: [ELEV]→[Nuka-Cola Bottler 3b]→[Nuka-Cola Bottler 3b]→[ELEV]
  • WEAPONS Floor 2 dwellers are the kill zone — equip with top weapons (Dragon's Maw, MIRV etc.)
REFERENCE: ROOM SIZES
Size in Blocks
Vault Door: 2 blocks (fixed)  |  Elevator: 1 square
PG / Diner / Water / Storage / LQ / Training: 1 block each — merge up to 3 blocks
Overseer's Office / Barbershop: 2 blocks (fixed, cannot expand)
Weapon / Outfit / Theme Workshop: 3 blocks (fixed, cannot shrink)
1 block = 3 squares  |  2 blocks = 6 sq  |  3 blocks = 9 sq
Capacity per Floor
Floor 1 (surface): VD (2 blocks) + up to 3 blocks of rooms + elevator = 17 squares
Underground floors: ELEV + 6 blocks of rooms + ELEV = 20 squares
